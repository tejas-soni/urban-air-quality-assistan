Air leaks around unsealed windows, attic hatches, and exterior doors force furnaces and electric heaters to work harder all winter while letting polluted outside air drift indoors continuously.

Weatherstripping windows and caulking exterior penetrations delivers a dual win: U.S. Department of Energy estimates show air sealing cuts residential winter heating and cooling demand by `~10–15%` while cutting uncontrolled outdoor `PM2.5` infiltration by `~25–40%`.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Air-seal exterior gaps before peak winter to reduce household heating energy by ~10–15% and cut outdoor smog infiltration by ~25–40% simultaneously.
