When transit isn't reachable for suburban school runs or office parks, combining three solo car journeys into one rotating 3-person carpool removes two entire vehicles from winter rush-hour traffic.

Because cold-start exhaust and brake/tire particulate wear scale per vehicle-kilometer traveled (`VKT`), sharing rides directly lowers both per-person `CO2e` and neighborhood roadside dust.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Rotate a 3-person school or office carpool across `10 km` daily trips to cut per-person `CO2e` and road particulate emissions by ~67% (`~2.2 kg CO2e/day` saved across the group).
