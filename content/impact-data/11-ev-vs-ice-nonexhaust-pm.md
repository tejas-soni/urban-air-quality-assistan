Switching from petrol/diesel to battery-electric vehicles eliminates tailpipe `NO2` and exhaust soot completely, yet OECD and UK AQEG analyses emphasize that tire wear, brake pad dust, and road dust resuspension now account for over `50%` of road-transport `PM2.5`/`PM10` in modern fleets.

Smooth driving, keeping tires properly inflated, using regenerative braking, and favoring rail/walking over heavy SUVs tackles both exhaust and non-exhaust urban particulates.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Pair electric/hybrid driving with regenerative braking and lighter mode shifts (rail/walk/bike) to reduce both tailpipe `NO2` (`100%`) and friction brake `PM` (`~50–70%` less brake wear).
