Three-way catalytic converters require temperatures above `250–350 °C` to oxidize hydrocarbons and reduce `NOx` efficiently. During sub-10 °C winter mornings, the first `2–5 km` after engine start release `2×–4×` higher particulate, carbon monoxide, and hydrocarbon emissions per kilometer than warm highway cruising.

Consolidating errands into a single warm trip ("trip chaining") or taking transit for short solo hops removes these cold-start pollution bursts from residential neighborhoods.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Chain errands into one trip or walk/transit for trips under `3 km` to avoid high winter cold-start emissions (~50–80% of short-trip tailpipe pollution).
