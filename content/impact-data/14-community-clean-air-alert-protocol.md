Ad-hoc scrambling on high-pollution mornings delays protective action. Community groups, PTA committees, and sports clubs achieve far higher adherence when they agree before winter on two clear message triggers:

- **Yellow/Orange Alert (`AQI 101+`):** Notify sensitive families, enable carpool/transit pledge, move strenuous kids' drills to `13:00–16:00`.
- **Red/Purple Alert (`AQI 151+`):** Shift outdoor PE/events indoors, turn on classroom `HEPA`/`Corsi-Rosenthal` units early, remind commuters to wear `N95`/`FFP2` and shift departures `30+ min` post-peak.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Adopt standardized `AQI 101` and `AQI 151` WhatsApp/bulletin templates so households and schools execute clean-room and anti-idling steps before morning rush peaks.
