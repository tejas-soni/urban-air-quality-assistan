Over half of urban errands and school drop-offs cover less than `5 km`. When driven by petrol or diesel car, these short winter trips emit disproportionate tailpipe pollutants per kilometer because engines run cold.

On Good or Moderate days (`AQI <=100`), or on USG days (`101–150`) with a side-street route and respirator for sensitive riders, shifting short `<5 km` trips to walking or an electric assist bike cuts direct tailpipe pollution to zero and trims lifecycle emissions by `~91–98%`.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Shift `4 km` daily errands from car to walk/e-bike on `AQI <=100` days (or quiet side streets) to cut trip emissions by ~91–98% (`~0.65 kg CO2e` saved per 4 km round-trip).
