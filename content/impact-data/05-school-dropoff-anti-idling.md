Dozens of cars idling outside a school gate for 15 minutes during winter morning drop-off or afternoon pickup create a concentrated micro-plume of `NO2`, cold-start `VOCs`, and `PM2.5` right at child breathing height.

Simple volunteer-led "Turn Your Key, Be Idle-Free" campaigns or moving pickup lines 100 meters away from playground doors eliminate stationary tailpipe clouds without requiring infrastructure budgets.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Implement a strict no-idling school gate pledge (`turn engine off if waiting >30 seconds`) to cut curbside `PM2.5` and `NO2` peaks by ~60–80%.
