Residential wood burning and uncertified solid-fuel stoves are a leading driver of neighborhood winter `PM2.5` spikes — one open fireplace evening emits roughly `50–150 grams` of fine smoke particles directly into adjacent backyards and bedrooms under calm inversion conditions.

Community "Spare the Air" alerts asking households with alternative electric/gas heat to pause decorative wood fires during `AQI 101+` inversions deliver immediate block-level relief.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Skip recreational wood fires on `AQI 101+` winter evenings to keep ~50–150 g of direct `PM2.5` wood smoke out of your immediate block's airshed.
