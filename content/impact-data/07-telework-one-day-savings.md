On forecasted high-smog mornings (`AQI 151+`), shifting even one weekly office commute day to remote work eliminates two peak-hour trips during the worst atmospheric inversion window.

Across a 5-day workweek, one telework day cuts weekly transport emissions and peak roadside `PM2.5`/`NO2` inhalation by an exact `20%`, while keeping household HVAC running inside a sealed clean space.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Work remotely on the worst forecasted smog day each week (`1 of 5 days`) to cut weekly commute emissions and roadside exposure by ~20%.
