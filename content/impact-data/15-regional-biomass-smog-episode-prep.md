Severe seasonal winter smog waves (from regional crop-residue burning, stagnant high-pressure systems, and urban heating) regularly trigger sudden local retail shortages of `HEPA` replacement cartridges, `MERV-13` furnace filters, and child/adult `N95`/`KN95` masks.

Checking filter condition before winter begins and stocking one spare filter set plus `5–10` fitted respirators per household member keeps protection uninterrupted throughout multi-week episodes.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Stock 1 spare `HEPA`/`MERV-13` filter pack and fitted `N95` respirators before peak winter smog so continuous `60–80%` indoor filtration never pauses mid-episode.
