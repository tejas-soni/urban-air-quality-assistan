Families sometimes hesitate to run HEPA purifiers 24/7 out of electricity cost or emissions concerns. Modern brushless DC portable HEPA cleaners consume roughly `20–45 watts` on medium sleep settings — comparable to a single laptop (`~0.42 kWh` for 12 hours, or roughly `0.15–0.20 kg CO2e/day` on an average grid).

The health benefit of reducing indoor `PM2.5` by `60–80%` across an entire winter episode vastly outweighs the modest energy use, especially when balanced by skipping one short car trip (`~0.5–1.5 kg CO2e`).

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Run a `~35 W` HEPA purifier `12–24 hours/day` on smog days (`~0.42–0.84 kWh/day`) to capture `~60–80%` of indoor `PM2.5` — offset its energy emissions tenfold by swapping one short car errand for transit or walking.
