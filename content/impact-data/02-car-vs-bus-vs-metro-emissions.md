Standard UK Government / DEFRA and US EPA transport conversion factors highlight stark per-passenger-kilometer (`pkm`) greenhouse and local pollutant differences:

- **Average solo petrol/diesel car:** `~170–192 g CO2e/pkm` plus cold-start tailpipe `NOx`/`PM`
- **Urban public bus:** `~89–105 g CO2e/pkm` (`~45–50%` lower per passenger)
- **Electric metro / light rail:** `~28–41 g CO2e/pkm` (`~78–83%` lower per passenger, zero street-level tailpipe `NO2`)
- **Walking / standard bicycle:** `0 g` tailpipe emissions (`~95%+` lifecycle savings)

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Replace a `10 km` round-trip solo car commute with electric metro/rail to save `~1.35 kg CO2e/day` (`~320 kg CO2e/year`) and eliminate tailpipe `NO2` along school/street corridors.
