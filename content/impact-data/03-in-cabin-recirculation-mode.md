Drivers sitting in stop-and-go queues pull tailpipe exhaust from the bumper ahead directly into vehicle vents when HVAC intake is set to "fresh/outside air". Switching the car climate system to **recirculation mode** paired with a clean cabin air filter cuts in-cabin ultrafine particles (`UFP`) and `PM2.5` by `~50–75%`.

For trips longer than `20–30 minutes` with multiple passengers, briefly crack windows or switch to fresh air for `30–60 seconds` on cleaner side-street stretches so exhaled `CO2` does not build up and cause drowsiness.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Engage cabin **recirculation** through congested highways and intersections to lower in-vehicle `PM2.5` and `UFP` exposure by ~50–75% (cycling fresh air briefly every 20–30 min).
