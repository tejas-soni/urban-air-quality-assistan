A "Walking School Bus" pairs two rotated parent volunteers with `10–20` neighborhood students along a fixed, quiet residential route `1–2 blocks` away from arterial avenues on days with `AQI <=100` (or `<=150` with optional child-sized KN95/KF94 masks).

Replacing 15 short `2 km` winter cold-start car drop-offs each morning removes congested school-gate queues and trims community school-run emissions dramatically.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Organize a 15-family Walking School Bus on `AQI <=100` days along quiet side streets to eliminate ~15 cold-start car trips daily (`~5–6 kg CO2e/day` saved + cleaner school gates).
