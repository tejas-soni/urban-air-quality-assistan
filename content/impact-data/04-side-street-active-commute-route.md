Walking or cycling immediately next to multi-lane bus and truck arterials puts pedestrians directly inside vehicle wake plumes. Mobile air monitoring campaigns consistently find that moving just `1–2 blocks` (`100–250 m`) onto parallel residential side streets cuts inhaled black carbon and `NO2` by `~20–35%` with minimal extra travel time.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Reroute walking and cycling commutes `1–2 blocks` off major arterial roads to cut black carbon and `PM2.5` exposure by ~20–35% per trip.
