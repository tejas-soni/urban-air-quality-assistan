Fine particles (`PM2.5`), nitrogen dioxide (`NO2`), and cold dry air irritate hyper-responsive airways, triggering wheezing and bronchospasm in children and adults with asthma. Symptoms can appear hours after exposure peaks.

Before leaving home on days with AQI `>100`, verify your reliever inhaler is packed, stay away from idling traffic queues, and spend sleep hours in a HEPA-filtered bedroom.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Keep AQI alerts active above `100`, follow your clinician's written Asthma Action Plan, and keep bedroom `PM2.5` below `15 µg/m³` to sharply lower overnight symptom flares.
