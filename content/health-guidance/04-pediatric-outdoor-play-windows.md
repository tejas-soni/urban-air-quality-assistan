Children are uniquely vulnerable to winter smog because their lungs are still developing and they breathe faster relative to body weight than adults — inhaling roughly `1.5×–2×` more air per kilogram during active play.

On days when AQI reaches `101–150` (Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups), shortening vigorous outdoor play and moving running games indoors prevents sharp spikes in deposited fine particulate dose.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Move kids' outdoor play to the lowest-AQI afternoon window (`13:00–16:00`) on Moderate/USG days (`51–150` AQI) or replace with indoor play above `150`, cutting peak `PM2.5` inhalation by ~35–50%.
