Traffic exhaust creates sharp spatial gradients of nitrogen dioxide (`NO2`) and ultrafine particles (`UFP`) immediately beside busy roadways. Air monitoring studies consistently show near-road `NO2` and black carbon concentrations decay steeply within `150–300 meters` downwind of arterial highways.

Choosing parallel neighborhood streets or placing school drop-off zones away from main roads reduces acute traffic-pollutant spikes for children and cyclists.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Walk or cycle 1–2 blocks parallel (`150+ m` away) from congested highways to cut roadside `NO2` and black carbon exposure by ~20–35%.
