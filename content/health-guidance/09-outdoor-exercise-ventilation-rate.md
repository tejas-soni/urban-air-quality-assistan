At rest, an adult breathes roughly `6–10 liters/minute`. During brisk cycling or running, minute ventilation jumps `3×–6×` (to `30–60+ L/min`), and mouth breathing bypasses nasal particle trapping, driving fine particulates deeper into lower airways.

Even a 45-minute jog at AQI `155` can deliver the same daily particulate dose as hours of sedentary outdoor exposure.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Switch high-intensity outdoor workouts to indoor filtered spaces whenever AQI exceeds `100` (sensitive) or `150` (everyone), cutting exercise `PM2.5` dose by ~75–85%.
