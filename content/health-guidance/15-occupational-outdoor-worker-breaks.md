Delivery riders, crossing guards, sanitation staff, and outdoor workers breathe elevated `PM2.5` for entire 8-hour shifts. Under NIOSH and state occupational smoke standards (e.g., Cal/OSHA Section 5141.1), employers trigger protective respirator provision when `PM2.5` AQI hits `151` or higher.

Even for independent commuters and gig riders, scheduled indoor breaks in clean spaces plus a fitted `N95`/`FFP2` dramatically lowers cumulative daily particle mass.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Provide `N95`/`FFP2` respirators at AQI `151+` and rotate heavy outdoor tasks indoors or into midday dispersion windows (`13:00–16:00`), cutting full-shift inhaled `PM2.5` by ~75–85%.
