Older adults and anyone with coronary artery disease, heart failure, or COPD experience measurable cardiovascular stress within hours of elevated `PM2.5` exposure (`>35.5 µg/m³`, AQI `101+`). Cold winter air compounds this by constricting airways and raising blood pressure.

Keeping indoor living spaces sealed during morning/evening peaks and running a properly sized HEPA purifier reduces indoor exposure while keeping gentle indoor mobility safe.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Keep seniors indoors with closed windows and HEPA filtration when AQI exceeds `100`, cutting `PM2.5` exposure by ~60–80% compared to ambient winter air.
