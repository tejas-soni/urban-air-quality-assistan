Fine particulate matter (`PM2.5`, particles 2.5 micrometers or smaller) penetrates deep into the lungs and enters the bloodstream. In 2021, the World Health Organization tightened its 24-hour `PM2.5` guideline from `25 µg/m³` to `15 µg/m³` and its annual guideline to `5 µg/m³`.

During severe winter smog, urban levels frequently exceed `75–250 µg/m³` (`5×` to `16×` the WHO 24-hour guideline). Comparing daily readings against the `15 µg/m³` WHO threshold reveals true health risk even when national AQI scales read "Moderate".

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Use `15 µg/m³` (24-hr) as your indoor target with filtration: keeping indoor `PM2.5` near `15 µg/m³` instead of `75 µg/m³` cuts daily inhaled particle mass by ~80%.
