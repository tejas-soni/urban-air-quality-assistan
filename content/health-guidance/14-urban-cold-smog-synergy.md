Cold, dry winter air (`<5 °C`) independently triggers reflex bronchoconstriction in people with asthma or sensitive lungs. When combined with winter smog `PM2.5` and `NO2`, the two stressors amplify coughing and airway narrowing during early morning school runs and commutes.

Wearing a well-fitted `N95`/`KN95` respirator both warms and humidifies incoming breaths slightly via retained moisture while still capturing `>=95%` of fine particulates.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Use a properly fitted `N95`/`KN95` rather than a loose cloth scarf alone to achieve `>=95%` aerosol filtration alongside warmer inhaled air on frigid morning walks.
