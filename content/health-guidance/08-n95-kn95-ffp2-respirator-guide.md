Cloth scarves and loose surgical masks allow fine `PM2.5` aerosols to bypass around the cheeks and nose bridge, often achieving only `20–50%` real-world inward filtration. Certified `N95`, `FFP2`, or `KN95` respirators use electrostatically charged melt-blown polypropylene fibers rated to capture `>=94–95%` of test aerosols down to `0.3 µm`.

Fit matters as much as filter media: press the nose wire flush across the bridge, confirm straps sit securely without gaps, and avoid facial hair along the sealing edge.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Wear a snug-fitting `N95`/`KN95`/`FFP2` outdoors when AQI exceeds `150` (or `100` for sensitive commuters), cutting inhaled `PM2.5` by up to `85–95%` compared to unmasked breathing.
