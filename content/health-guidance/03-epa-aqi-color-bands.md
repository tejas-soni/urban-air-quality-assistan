The US EPA Air Quality Index (AQI) translates pollutant concentrations onto a `0–500` scale across six standardized categories: Good (`0–50`), Moderate (`51–100`), Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups (`101–150`), Unhealthy (`151–200`), Very Unhealthy (`201–300`), and Hazardous (`301+`).

In 2024, the US EPA lowered the upper `PM2.5` threshold for the Good (`0–50`) category from `12.0 µg/m³` to `9.0 µg/m³` to reflect stronger cardiovascular and respiratory evidence.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Trigger protective shifts at AQI `101+` (`>35.4 µg/m³` PM2.5) for children, older adults, and people with asthma; shift all vigorous outdoor activity indoors at AQI `151+` (`>55.4 µg/m³`).
