Sustained exposure to high `PM2.5` levels is associated in epidemiological studies with low birth weight and preterm birth risk. While short exposure spikes cannot always be eliminated, consistent low-cost habits significantly lower cumulative particulate burden across trimesters.

Prioritizing well-filtered indoor sleeping quarters, avoiding unvented cooking fumes, and choosing off-peak or side-street walks keep daily inhalation closer to WHO targets.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Combine a bedroom HEPA/Corsi-Rosenthal clean zone overnight (~60–80% lower `PM2.5`) with off-peak errands (`13:00–16:00`) to cut daily inhaled particulate load substantially.
