On clear winter nights, ground surfaces cool rapidly, forming a cold, dense air layer trapped beneath warmer air aloft — a temperature inversion. Emissions from morning rush-hour traffic and residential heating accumulate in this shallow lid between `06:00` and `10:00` and again after `18:00`.

Solar warming around midday breaks or lifts the inversion layer between roughly `13:00` and `16:00`, dispersing ground-level `PM2.5` and `NO2` into a deeper air column.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Schedule outdoor errands, school walks, and brief cross-ventilation between `13:00` and `16:00` instead of `07:00–09:00` for ~30–45% lower ambient `PM2.5` concentration.
