During Very Unhealthy (`201–300`) and Hazardous (`301+`) AQI events, fine particulates can trigger acute inflammatory responses, blood pressure spikes, and airway constriction. Vulnerable individuals may feel unusual shortness of breath, persistent cough, wheezing, dizziness, or chest tightness.

Never wait out worsening chest pain or severe breathlessness — treat acute symptoms as a medical concern while moving immediately into clean, filtered indoor air.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Move indoors immediately into a filtered room (`~60–80%` lower `PM2.5`) at the first sign of respiratory distress and seek prompt medical care for chest pain or persistent wheezing.
