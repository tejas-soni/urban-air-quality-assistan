Schools can protect hundreds of students simultaneously by adopting clear, pre-agreed AQI thresholds rather than making ad-hoc decisions at bell time. The US EPA Air Quality Flag Program defines exact actions per band:

- **Yellow (51–100):** Watch sensitive students closely during strenuous activity.
- **Orange (101–150):** Shorten intense outdoor PE; move asthma-prone students indoors as needed.
- **Red (151–200):** Move all vigorous activities indoors or reschedule; keep outdoor time light.
- **Purple (201+):** Move all recess, sports, and dismissal waiting indoors with portable air cleaners.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Shifting PE indoors at AQI `151+` with MERV-13/HEPA classroom filtration cuts student exercise `PM2.5` dose by ~65–80%.
