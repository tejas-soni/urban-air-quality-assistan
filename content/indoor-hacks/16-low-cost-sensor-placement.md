Consumer laser-scattering `PM2.5` monitors help families verify whether their filtration actually works, yet placement errors distort readings. Placing a monitor right beside an ultrasonic humidifier, steam kettle, or exterior draft exaggerates particle scatter (`>70%` relative humidity swells aerosols hygroscopically).

Mount sensors at seated breathing height (`1–1.5 m`) away from kitchens and bathrooms, and compare indoor readings against your city's reference outdoor reading.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Place home `PM2.5` monitors at `1–1.5 m` height away from steam/humidifiers to accurately verify when indoor filtration reaches `<=15 µg/m³`.
