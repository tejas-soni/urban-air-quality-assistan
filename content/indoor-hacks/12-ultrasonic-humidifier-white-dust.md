Dry winter indoor heating pushes families to run humidifiers, yet ultrasonic cool-mist units vibrate dissolved calcium and magnesium from tap water straight into the air — causing indoor optical sensors and lungs to register `PM2.5` spikes of `50–200+ µg/m³`.

Using distilled/reverse-osmosis water in ultrasonic humidifiers — or switching to wick-based evaporative humidifiers — eliminates aerosolized mineral dust.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Fill ultrasonic humidifiers only with distilled water (or use evaporative wick units) to prevent `>95%` of humidifier-generated mineral `PM2.5` spikes.
