In leaky winter apartments, polluted outdoor air continuously seeps through sash gaps, balcony sliders, and under exterior doors via stack effect and wind pressure — making purifiers work twice as hard.

Applying low-cost adhesive EPDM foam weatherstripping around window frames and installing a rubber door sweep takes under an hour with zero tools and immediately slows incoming smog leakage.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Seal exterior window and door gaps with foam gaskets and sweeps to reduce uncontrolled smog infiltration by ~25–40%, helping indoor purifiers maintain `<=15 µg/m³`.
