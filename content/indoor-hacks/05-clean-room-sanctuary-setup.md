When whole-home filtration isn't affordable during AQI `200+` emergencies, concentrating filtration inside one primary bedroom creates a reliable sleep and recovery sanctuary. Choose a room with minimal exterior window area and an interior door you can keep closed.

Keep windows latched, place a sized HEPA or DIY Corsi-Rosenthal cleaner inside, forbid candles/smoking/sprays, and prioritize infants, elderly family members, and anyone with asthma sleeping in that clean zone.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Establish one sealed clean-air bedroom running continuous filtration overnight to maintain `~75–85%` lower `PM2.5` for 8+ hours of daily recovery.
