In classrooms (`70–90 m²`), a single purifier tucked against a back wall leaves dead zones near desks. Deploying two distributed portable `HEPA` or `DIY Corsi-Rosenthal` units toward opposite central edges creates uniform mixing and cuts noise per unit.

Pairing portable cleaners with sealed exterior corridor doors during severe winter smog protects 25–35 students simultaneously.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Deploy two distributed portable air cleaners per standard classroom (`>=4.8 ACH` combined) to cut student school-day `PM2.5` exposure by ~50–70%.
