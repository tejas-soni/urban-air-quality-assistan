Street shoes and heavy winter coats collect tire-wear particulates, brake dust, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), and coarse `PM10` soil along winter sidewalks. Once tracked onto rugs and upholstery, children crawling or walking stir those particles repeatedly into indoor air.

Placing a washable doormat, dedicated shoe rack, and coat hook right inside the entrance stops tracked particulate transfer before it reaches bedrooms.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Adopt a strict shoes-off and coat-hanging zone at the front door to reduce indoor tracked road dust and settled `PM10` resuspension.
