Standard residential furnace filters (`MERV 6–8`) are designed to protect blower coils from lint, capturing less than `20%` of fine `0.3–1.0 µm` winter smog particles. Under `ASHRAE Standard 52.2`, `MERV-13` pleated filters capture `>=50%` of `0.3–1.0 µm` aerosols and `>=85%` of `1–3 µm` particles per pass.

Before upgrading, verify your furnace manual supports `MERV-13` pressure drop (thicker `4-inch` media filters have lower airflow resistance than `1-inch` ones) and set the thermostat fan to "ON" during smog alerts.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Upgrade compatible central HVAC slots to `MERV-13` and circulate continuously during smog alerts to capture `>=50%` of fine particles per pass vs `<20%` on `MERV-8`.
