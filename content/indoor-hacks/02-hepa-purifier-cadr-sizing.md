A portable air cleaner only reduces room `PM2.5` reliably if its Clean Air Delivery Rate (`CADR`) matches the room volume. The AHAM `2/3 rule` recommends a smoke CADR in `cfm` equal to at least two-thirds of the room's floor area in square feet (targeting `~4.8` Air Changes per Hour).

True HEPA H13 media captures `99.97%` of `0.3 µm` particles per pass, and avoiding ionizer/ozone-generating add-ons prevents secondary indoor ozone creation.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Run a properly sized True HEPA purifier continuously at medium/high speed (`~4.8 ACH`) to cut indoor `PM2.5` by ~60–80% during high-AQI episodes.
