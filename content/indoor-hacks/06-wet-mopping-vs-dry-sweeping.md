Settled indoor dust contains resuspended `PM10`, heavy metals, and tracked-in combustion soot. Dry brooms and unsealed vacuum cleaners kick fine particles back into breathing height (`1–1.5 m`), spiking indoor `PM2.5`/`PM10` for over an hour.

Using damp microfiber mops, damp dusting cloths, and `HEPA`-sealed vacuum cleaners captures settled particles into water or sealed bags rather than re-aerosolizing them.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Replace dry sweeping with damp microfiber mopping and HEPA vacuuming on smog days to prevent `50–150 µg/m³` indoor resuspension spikes.
