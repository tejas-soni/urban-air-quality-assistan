Humans spend roughly one-third of every day sleeping, making bedroom air quality the single highest-leverage target for lowering 24-hour `PM2.5` intake. Placing an air purifier `>=0.5–1 meter` clear of curtains and walls preserves unobstructed airflow loops.

Running a larger-CADR purifier continuously on a quieter medium or low setting maintains `<15 µg/m³` all night without disrupting sleep.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Position your purifier away from curtains/corners and run it continuously through sleep hours to protect ~8 hours (`33%` of your day) at WHO-target `<=15 µg/m³`.
