Opening windows at `07:00–09:00` coincides with both morning commuter emissions and shallow winter boundary-layer temperature inversions — drawing peak outdoor `PM2.5` straight into bedrooms and living rooms.

By contrast, solar heating lifts the atmospheric mixing height between `13:00` and `16:00`, typically producing the lowest daytime ambient `PM2.5` window before evening heating and traffic rebuild the night inversion. When outdoor AQI is `<150`, brief `10–15 minute` cross-ventilation around `14:00` refreshes indoor `CO2` while letting far fewer particles enter; above AQI `150`, keep windows shut and rely on mechanical filtration.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Ventilate for `10–15 minutes` around `14:00` instead of `08:00` (or keep sealed above AQI `150`) to reduce incoming outdoor `PM2.5` intake by ~35–45%.
