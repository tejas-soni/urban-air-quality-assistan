High-heat pan-frying above an oil's smoke point emits dense ultrafine aerosols and aldehydes, while unvented gas burners emit substantial `NO2` into tight winter homes.

Covering pans with lids, favoring steaming, pressure cooking, or simmering on high-smog days, and using portable single-burner induction cooktops instead of unvented gas keeps indoor air breathable when windows cannot stay open.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Switch high-smog-day meals to covered steaming/simmering or portable electric induction to eliminate stove `NO2` (~100% burner NO2 cut) and halve cooking `PM2.5`.
