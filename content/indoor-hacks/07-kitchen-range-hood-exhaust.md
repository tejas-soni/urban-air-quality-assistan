Frying, roasting, and gas-burner cooking often produce indoor `PM2.5` spikes (`100–400 µg/m³`) higher than outdoor winter smog — especially when windows stay closed! Gas stoves also release nitrogen dioxide (`NO2`) directly into living areas.

Running an externally ducted range hood (`>=100–250 cfm`) before lighting burners, using back burners (better hood capture plume), and leaving the fan on for `10–15 minutes` afterward removes pollutants at the source.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Run a ducted range hood on back burners during and `15 minutes` after cooking to cut indoor `PM2.5` and `NO2` peaks by ~55–80%.
