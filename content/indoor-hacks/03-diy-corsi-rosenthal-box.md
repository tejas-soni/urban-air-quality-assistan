Commercial HEPA units can be expensive for large living rooms or classrooms. A DIY Corsi-Rosenthal Box — four `20×20 inch` `MERV-13` pleated furnace filters taped into a cube with a cardboard bottom, topped by a modern UL-listed `20-inch` box fan and simple triangular cardboard shroud — delivers `~500–850 cfm` of clean air.

Controlled chamber tests by UC Davis, Underwriters Laboratories (UL), and US EPA researchers demonstrated `PM2.5` reductions comparable to or exceeding mid-range commercial HEPA cleaners at roughly one-third the cost.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Build a 4-filter `MERV-13` Corsi-Rosenthal box (~`$65–80`) for living rooms or classrooms to achieve `~70–85%` indoor `PM2.5` reduction at `~500+ cfm` CADR.
