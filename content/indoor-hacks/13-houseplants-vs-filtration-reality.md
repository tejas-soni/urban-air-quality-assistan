While indoor greenery enhances wellbeing, peer-reviewed reviews show potted houseplants remove trace gases at negligible rates in real homes: achieving the equivalent clean-air exchange of a single portable `HEPA` unit would require `10–1,000` plants per square meter, and plants do not filter `PM2.5` meaningfully.

Rely on mechanical `HEPA` or `MERV-13` filtration for real `PM2.5` protection during winter smog.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Do not rely on houseplants for smog filtration; use a mechanical `HEPA` or `MERV-13` filter (`100×+` higher particle removal rate) to hit `<=15 µg/m³`.
