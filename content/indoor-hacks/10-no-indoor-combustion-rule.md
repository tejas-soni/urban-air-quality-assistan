When outdoor windows are shut to block winter smog, burning incense sticks, scented paraffin candles, tobacco, or unvented kerosene/biomass heaters traps intense combustion aerosols inside the house. A single incense stick in an unventilated bedroom can drive `PM2.5` past `150–300 µg/m³` within minutes.

Keep indoor air strictly combustion-free whenever ambient AQI exceeds `50`.

## Key Action & Quantified Impact
- Eliminate indoor incense, candles, and smoking during closed-window smog days to avoid adding `100–300+ µg/m³` of self-generated indoor `PM2.5`.
